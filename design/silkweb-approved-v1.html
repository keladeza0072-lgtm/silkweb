GOOD TO KNOW

Common questions.

What can SilkWeb help me create?

SilkWeb is being built to help create advertising concepts, campaign copy, original imagery, and responsive landing pages from a short brief. Availability depends on the connected provider.

Do I need design experience?

No. Start by describing what you want in everyday language. You can refine your direction as you go.

Does SilkWeb generate real images?

Original image generation is planned for the MVP but is not connected in this standalone frontend reference. Example artwork is illustrative.

Can I edit what SilkWeb creates?

The intended workflow lets you choose a concept, refine the copy, and preview the corresponding website. Live integration is the next engineering step.
