# SilkWeb Creative — Active Intelligence Library v1

## Scope
The full advertising taxonomy contains 42 styles.
Only six styles are active in the hackathon MVP.

These six are implementation selections for the MVP.
Maintain stable identifiers and allow future expansion.

## Advertising Styles

### problem-solution
Name: Problem vs. Solution
Purpose: Connect an identifiable problem to a relevant solution.
Composition: Problem hook, product introduction, solution visual, CTA.
Copy: Empathetic and benefit-led.
Restriction: Never invent pain points, efficacy, or product results.

### notification-offer
Name: Offer / Notification Offer
Purpose: Highlight a genuine promotional incentive.
Composition: Notification-inspired visual, prominent offer, clear CTA.
Copy: Concise, specific, urgency only when substantiated.
Restriction: Only use promotions explicitly supplied by the user.
Without a verified offer, recommend another style.

### chat-script
Name: WhatsApp / Chat Script
Purpose: Present advertising as a relatable conversation.
Composition: Chat-style bubbles, clear dialogue progression, CTA.
Copy: Natural, conversational, concise.
Restriction: Do not imply fabricated messages are genuine testimonials.
Avoid copying protected platform branding unnecessarily.

### whiteboard
Name: Whiteboard
Purpose: Explain a product, process, or service.
Composition: Structured diagrams, short labels, clear visual hierarchy.
Copy: Educational, direct, logically sequenced.
Restriction: Do not invent numerical claims or technical capabilities.

### before-after
Name: Before vs. After
Purpose: Contrast a situation, process, or experience.
Composition: Clear two-state comparison.
Copy: Transformation-focused with substantiated benefits.
Restriction: Do not fabricate outcomes, physical transformations,
performance improvements, or guaranteed results.

### editorial
Name: Editorial / Magazine
Purpose: Premium photography-led brand storytelling.
Composition: Hero photography, sophisticated typography,
generous negative space, carefully placed product and CTA.
Copy: Elegant, concise, emotionally resonant.
Restriction: Do not fabricate luxury certifications, endorsements,
ingredients, product properties, or brand history.

## Copywriting Frameworks

### aida
Attention -> Interest -> Desire -> Action
Use when introducing an offering and guiding interest
toward a clear next action.

### pas
Problem -> Agitation -> Solution
Use for recognizable customer problems.
Agitation must not rely on fearmongering or fabricated claims.

### bab
Before -> After -> Bridge
Use for relevant, supportable transformations.
After states must not imply unsupported outcomes.

### fab
Features -> Advantages -> Benefits
Use when actual product features are provided.
Never fabricate features or benefits.

## Creative Selection Rules
1. Select styles based on product, objective, channel, and tone.
2. Treat the user's chosen style as a preference when compatible.
3. Recommend a different style when required evidence is absent.
4. Generate two genuinely distinct creative concepts.
5. Use compatible copywriting frameworks.
6. Explain recommendations in simple user-facing language.
7. Do not require users to understand advertising terminology.
8. Maintain supplied facts separately from creative assumptions.
9. Keep photography prompts aligned with real product facts.
10. Perform policy review separately from creative-readiness scoring.

## Future Expansion
The remaining 36 advertising styles are outside MVP scope.
Do not fabricate their definitions or activate them automatically.
