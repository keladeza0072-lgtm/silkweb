# SILKWEB CREATIVE — FINAL MVP DESIGN CONTRACT

## Product experience
An AI creative agency inside a browser.
One connected journey:
Describe business -> Refine brief -> Explore two ads -> Select -> Preview website.

## Visual direction
Premium editorial design, not a SaaS dashboard.
Palette:
Blue #0593C6
White #FCFFFF
Pink #D83995
Dark ink #142238

White-dominant, expressive typography, deliberate asymmetric composition,
generous breathing room, strong image-led presentation.

## Homepage
- SilkWeb wordmark, minimal navigation.
- Large editorial headline: "One idea. An entire campaign."
- One prominent conversational prompt composer.
- A visually compelling example campaign area.
- No six-section navigation or empty feature cards.

## Workspace
- Left: compact conversation and campaign context.
- Right: large creative canvas.
- Progressive disclosure: show only relevant tools.
- No numbered wizard or long explanatory paragraphs.
- No technical API terminology visible to customers.

## Concept experience
- Exactly two distinctive advertising concepts.
- Creative artwork dominates each concept.
- Headline, copy, CTA, style and framework accessible in an inspector.
- Selecting a concept updates the campaign workspace.
- Never display fake generated images.

## Website experience
- Selected concept becomes a responsive landing-page preview.
- Keep campaign copy and imagery consistent.
- Desktop/mobile preview modes.
- Use only supplied product facts.

## Evaluation
- Seven transparent weighted readiness criteria.
- Separate policy-risk checks.
- Compact inspector, not an entire dashboard page.
- Never claim Meta approval.

## Implementation
- Reuse existing backend, tests, creative library and endpoints.
- No paid inference without explicit approval.
- No fake model output.
- No unrelated refactors.
- Mobile responsive.
- Complete browser review required.

## Acceptance
A first-time user understands the product immediately,
can enter a simple skincare brief, review what SilkWeb understood,
explore real concepts when inference is configured,
and see a polished website preview.
